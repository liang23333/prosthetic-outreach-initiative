# Prosthetic Outreach Initiative

A collaborative initiative to deliver affordable, high-quality prosthetic solutions through community outreach, open innovation, and evidence-based clinical practices.

---

## 🔬 Clinical Trial References & Evidence Base

Our approach builds directly on recent clinical research in low-cost and scalable prosthetics:

1. **NCT04725461 - Low Cost Socket for Lower Limb Amputees**
   - *Institution:* Shirley Ryan AbilityLab (Chicago, IL)
   - *Key Innovation:* Rapid socket fitting utilizing expandable rigid polyurethane foam (formed by mixing two liquid components) within a 3D-printed outer shell, cutting casting and socket completion to under 90 minutes.
   - *Application:* Scalable, rapid field fittings for lower-limb amputees in resource-constrained communities.

2. **NCT05220553 - PROLIMB: Sensorimotor Prosthesis for the Upper Limb**
   - *Institutions:* University Hospitals Coventry and Warwickshire NHS Trust, University of Warwick, University College London
   - *Key Innovation:* Affordable body-powered prosthetic fingertip digits with mechanical haptic/sensory feedback and 3D-printed components.
   - *Application:* High-dexterity, low-maintenance partial hand and finger prosthetic solutions.

3. **NCT04700085 - ReMotion Prosthetic Knee (Reverse Innovation in Healthcare)**
   - *Institutions:* Sint Maartenskliniek & D-Rev
   - *Key Innovation:* Low-cost ($80) polycentric mechanical knee with standard ISO 10328 durability, engineered specifically for rugged use in underserved populations.
   - *Application:* Robust above-knee prosthetic systems at a fraction of commercial costs.

## 👥 Regional Hubs & Coordinators

We maintain three initial coordinator nodes across the United States:
- **Chicago Node (Midwest):** Device prototyping, rapid fabrication, and socket testing.
- **Boston Node (Northeast):** Clinical partnerships, biomechanics research, and volunteer training.
- **Atlanta Node (Southeast):** Community outreach, patient advocacy, and rural clinic partnerships.
